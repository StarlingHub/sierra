Complete Product Information

Solidifier® Disposable Spit Cup is an innovative personal and medical hygiene solution designed to eliminate public spitting and messy bedside receptacles. Each 450ml cup comes pre-loaded with our fast-acting solidifying formula that instantly solidifies liquid bio-waste on contact.

Whether you are travelling long distances by car or flight, recovering from illness at home, managing respiratory/TB care, or looking for a discreet, hygienic tobacco spit cup, Solidifier® ensures zero leakage, zero odour, and stress-free disposal.

  1. Open the Lid: Remove the top lid from the Solidifier® Spit Cup. Do not remove the white solidifying powder inside the cup.
  2. Spit Into the Cup: Use the cup as needed. Each spit begins solidifying within seconds.
  3. Re-cap Between Uses: Place the snug lid back on between uses to lock in hygiene and prevent accidental knocks.
  4. Dispose Safely: Once full (up to 450ml / ~50 spits), secure the lid firmly and dispose of the cup in a dry waste dustbin.
