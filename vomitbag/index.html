Complete Product Information

Solidifier® Disposable Vomit Bag is a leak-proof emergency emesis pouch engineered to solidify liquid bio-waste immediately. Ideal for motion sickness, car sickness, air travel, pregnancy nausea, hospital wards, and outdoor emergencies.

Unlike flimsy plastic bags that leak or emit unpleasant odours, Solidifier® features a reinforced gusseted pouch, an internal solidifying formula, and an adhesive peel-and-fold seal so you can carry or dispose of it with complete peace of mind.

  1. Open the Pouch: Pull the top opening wide apart. Leave the solidifying agent inside the bag.
  2. Use During Nausea: Hold the wide rim securely around the mouth. Liquid begins solidifying immediately.
  3. Peel & Fold to Seal: Remove the protective paper strip along the top flap, fold the top down, and press firmly to create a leak-proof seal.
  4. Dispose Hygienically: Discard the sealed pouch into a waste bin.
